# How CISA scoring works

Short version: ISACA reports your result on a scale from 200 to 800, and 450 or higher is a pass. The score depends only on how many scored questions you answer correctly. There is no penalty for wrong answers, so never leave a question blank.

## The scaled score

ISACA converts your raw score, the number of scored questions you got right, onto a common scale so that results from different versions of the exam are comparable and fair.

| Score | Meaning, per ISACA |
|---|---|
| 800 | A perfect score: all questions answered correctly |
| 450 or higher | A pass, which ISACA describes as the minimum standard of knowledge |
| 200 | The lowest possible score: only a small number answered correctly |

A score of 450 does **not** mean 45% of questions answered correctly. ISACA does not publish the raw-to-scaled conversion, and the raw score needed can differ between versions of the exam.

## Pretest items

ISACA exams contain scored items and pretest items. Pretest items are being trialled for future exams and are not used to calculate your score. ISACA does not publish how many there are or which ones they are, so treat every question as if it counts.

## Domains do not have their own pass marks

Your score is based on the total number of scored items you answer correctly, regardless of domain. ISACA gives domain-level results for information only. The domain percentages show how much of the exam each domain represents; they are not used to calculate the score. So a weak domain can be offset by strong ones, but with Domains 4 and 5 at 26% each, a weak result in either is hard to make up.

## No penalty for guessing

ISACA's candidate guide says there are no penalties for incorrect answers and that grades are based solely on the number of questions answered correctly. Answer every question, even if you have to guess.

## Getting your result

- At the end of the exam you see your **preliminary** pass or fail status on screen. Screenshots and photos of the results screen are not allowed.
- The **official score** is emailed and posted online, in MyISACA, within ten working days.
- ISACA does not provide question-level results.

## Rescoring

If you do not pass, you can request a rescore in writing through ISACA support within 30 days of the results being released. Rescores are performed by PSI, and a fee applies; check the current guide for details.

## Retakes

ISACA allows four attempts within a rolling 12-month period.

| Attempt | Wait |
|---|---|
| 2 | 30 days after attempt 1 |
| 3 | 90 days after attempt 2 |
| 4 | 90 days after attempt 3 |

Each attempt needs a full registration. If you pass, you cannot retake the same exam during the five-year window to apply for certification.

## What counts as good practice performance

No practice test can reproduce ISACA's scale, so no practice percentage converts to a 450. As our study guidance, not an ISACA standard:

- Use practice sets to find **which domains** cost you points.
- Review every miss: why is the key better than the option you chose?
- Aim to be consistent across Domains 4 and 5, since they carry half the score.
