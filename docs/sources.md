## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice, not ISACA statements:

- the per-question timing targets and checkpoints
- the approximate question counts per domain, which are arithmetic on the published weights
- the methods, traps lists, key concepts summaries and study plans
- all practice items and explanations, which are original

General audit and IT concepts such as sampling methods, backup types, recovery sites and encryption are standard professional knowledge, described here in our own words.
