# Test-day checklist

A short list for the day before, the morning of the exam, and afterwards. Your PSI confirmation and ISACA's candidate guide set the rules that apply to you; follow them where they differ from anything here.

## The day before

- Confirm your appointment, your delivery mode (test centre or remote) and the start time. Your registration has a six-month eligibility window, so make sure the date falls inside it.
- For a test centre: check the address and travel time. For remote proctoring: check your computer, webcam, connection and a quiet, clear room against PSI's requirements.
- Check that your identification matches your registration exactly.
- Read the traps lists in the domain guides. Nothing new.
- Fix the [timing checkpoints](timing.md#a-pacing-plan) in your head.
- Stop studying by early evening and sleep.

## The morning of the exam

- Eat something that will last four hours.
- Arrive early, or log in early for a remote exam.
- Leave behind anything not allowed: phones, smartwatches, notes. Devices in the testing area can end your exam.

## In the first minutes

- Use the tutorial at the start. ISACA's guide says to pay close attention to it so you do not miss important information about how the exam works.
- Note the time remaining so the checkpoints make sense.

## During the exam

- Read the last sentence of each question first, then the stem.
- Ask: what would the IS auditor do? Evaluate, evidence, report.
- Answer every question. There is no penalty for wrong answers.
- Do not spend more than about two minutes on one question.
- A short break of up to ten minutes is allowed with the proctor's permission, but the timer keeps running.

## Afterwards

- You see a preliminary pass or fail on screen. Do not photograph or screenshot it; it is not allowed.
- The official score arrives by email and in MyISACA within ten working days.
- **If you passed:** apply for certification within five years, with experience gained in the ten years before your application. If you do not yet have the experience, look at the CISA Associate designation. See the [financial auditors page](for-financial-auditors.md#experience-waivers-and-the-application).
- **If you did not pass:** you can request a rescore within 30 days. To retake, wait 30 days after a first attempt and 90 days after a second or third, up to four attempts in 12 months. Use your domain results to target study.

## Last practice before the real one

A final timed run a few days before, not the night before, helps you trust your pace. The [free CISA practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/cisa?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=cisa) is one option.
