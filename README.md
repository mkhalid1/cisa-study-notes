# CISA Study Notes

A free, structured study guide for the ISACA CISA exam: how the exam works and is scored, a guide to each of the five domains with the key concepts examiners test, 20 original practice items with full explanations, a 5-item diagnostic, and plans for your final week or final two days. Written for IT auditors, risk and security professionals, and financial auditors and accountants moving into IT audit, who get a page of their own. Facts about the exam come only from ISACA's own pages, each one [cited](docs/sources.md).

Want to see where you stand first? Take the [free timed CISA practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/cisa?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=6n8cun&utm_content=cisa), or the shorter [5-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md) in this repo.

## Start here

1. **Find your weak domains.** Take the [5-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md). It has one item from each domain and tells you where to start.
2. **Fix the weakest domain first.** Open its guide below, learn the key concepts and the method, then do its practice items.
3. **Follow a plan.** Use the [7-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#7-day-plan) for your final week, or the [2-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#2-day-plan) if the exam is close.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Publisher | ISACA |
| Questions | 150 multiple choice, including unscored pretest items |
| Time | 4 hours |
| Score | Scaled, 200 to 800 |
| Pass | 450 or higher |
| Wrong answers | No penalty. Answer everything. |
| Delivery | PSI test centres or remote proctoring |
| Languages | English, Chinese (Simplified), Spanish, Japanese, French, German, Korean |
| Experience to certify | 5 years, with waivers of up to 3 years. Apply within 5 years of passing. |
| Pass rate | Not published by ISACA |

Sources: [ISACA content outline](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cisa/cisa-exam-content-outline) and the [ISACA Certification Exam Candidate Guide](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/-/media/fa494652c5f149289af38cef18328650.ashx), checked 24 November 2026.

## The five domains at a glance

| Domain | Weight | What it is really about | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Information Systems Auditing Process | 18% | Planning, sampling, evidence, analytics, reporting, independence | [Domain 1](docs/sections/information-systems-auditing-process.md) |
| 2. Governance and Management of IT | 18% | Strategy, committees, policy, risk, vendors, performance | [Domain 2](docs/sections/governance-and-management-of-it.md) |
| 3. IS Acquisition, Development and Implementation | 12% | Business cases, projects, controls, testing, migration, go-live | [Domain 3](docs/sections/acquisition-development-and-implementation.md) |
| 4. IS Operations and Business Resilience | 26% | Operations, change, incidents, backups, continuity and recovery | [Domain 4](docs/sections/operations-and-business-resilience.md) |
| 5. Protection of Information Assets | 26% | Access, networks, encryption, cloud, monitoring, incidents, forensics | [Domain 5](docs/sections/protection-of-information-assets.md) |

Every domain guide has the same parts: the official topics, key concepts, how the questions look, a step-by-step method, the traps that cost points, timing advice, original practice items with explanations (4 in each domain), and a score table.

## How to use this repo

- **Read on GitHub.** Every page has links to the previous page, the next page and back to these contents, so you can read it start to finish like a book.
- **Test yourself honestly.** Answers are hidden under "Answers and explanations". Commit to an answer before you open them.
- **Read every explanation,** including for items you got right. Each one says why the nearest wrong option loses, which is where CISA points are won.
- **Think as the auditor.** Most wrong answers on the CISA are things management should do. The [exam overview](docs/test-overview.md#the-auditors-point-of-view) explains the point of view.
- **Keep a miss log.** The [study plans](docs/study-plans.md#how-to-review-a-missed-item) show how.
- **Practise timed.** A timed run, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/cisa?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=6n8cun&utm_content=cisa), trains the pace.
- **Clone or fork it** to annotate your own copy. The structure also works as a GitBook space: see [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md).

## About the practice items

All 20 domain practice items and 5 diagnostic items are original, written for these notes. None are taken from ISACA, its review manuals or question databases, or any prep site. They follow ISACA's published content outline and its description of how CISA items are written, so the reasoning you learn here transfers.
